What Alpha Finder looks for

Indian listed businesses with a unique product, service, technology, distribution advantage, or execution capability whose earnings power may not yet be fully reflected in the price.

Universe

Primary hunting ground: below ₹1,000 Cr market cap. The active scanner ceiling is ₹3,000 Cr; use ₹3,500 Cr only as a deliberate expansion. Crossing ₹1,000 Cr does not invalidate a good business.

Evidence required

  1. Market cap reconciled as latest price × diluted shares outstanding, with a date and source.
  2. Original concall read: distinguish management statements from analyst questions and track prior guidance.
  3. Order book checked for amount, delivery period, binding nature and revenue-recognition path.
  4. Governance, cash conversion, working capital, dilution, pledge, auditor and liquidity risks reviewed.

Valuation

Trailing P/E describes present earnings. Forward P/E tests a sourced future case:

Revenue × OPM → EBITDA; + normalised other income − interest − depreciation → PBT; − tax → PAT; ÷ diluted shares → forward EPS; price ÷ forward EPS → forward P/E.

Use separate bear, base and bull cases. Every assumption must have a filing/concall source and page reference.
